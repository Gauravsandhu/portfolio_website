## What it is

[Drawabox](https://drawabox.com) is a free course that teaches drawing by building the fundamentals first: confident lines, ellipses and boxes, then using them to draw plants, insects, animals and objects. Most of the work is drawing from the shoulder, sticking to ink so mistakes stay on the page, and doing a lot of boxes. The 250 Box Challenge is famous for a reason.

## Why I started it

I make games and 3D models, and I kept running into the same wall: I couldn't sketch an idea quickly or make my own 2D art for sprites and concepts. Drawabox teaches you to think in 3D forms on paper, which ties straight back into modelling in Blender.
